I’m a security researcher and developer based in Washington, DC. I work on anonymity networks, internet censorship, and cellular surveillance.

I founded Cerura Systems, a counter-surveillance company. Its first product, CANARY, is a sensor network that detects fake cell towers (IMSI catchers) around sensitive sites, where they can silently track and intercept every phone nearby.

Before Cerura, I worked on the other side of the same problem: states that turn the internet against their own people. I built [BLACKOUT](https://blackout.scsp.ai/), which tracks internet shutdowns and censorship worldwide and is now hosted by SCSP, and spent some time on [Arti](https://gitlab.torproject.org/tpo/core/arti/-/work_items?sort=created_date&state=all&author_username=moumenalaoui&first_page_size=20), Tor’s Rust client.

Outside of work, I fly drones, DJ, train BJJ, and go target shooting. I've also had a soft spot for reptiles and arachnids for as long as I can remember. 

I write occasionally under [Writing](/writing/), and the easiest way to reach me is at [moumenalaoui@proton.me](mailto:moumenalaoui@proton.me).
